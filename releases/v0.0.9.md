## Quest Progress HUD — initial public beta

A compact client-side HUD for your team's FTB Quests progress:

```text
Quests    508 / 4790    10.6%
[thin green progress bar]
```

### Features

- Completed / total counts and percentage on one line, with a thin progress bar.
- One-second progress refresh, with no full quest scan while the HUD is hidden.
- Loading state while quest or team data is syncing; stale data cleared when disconnecting.
- Saved settings for visibility, four screen corners, offsets, scale, and background opacity.
- Standard Minecraft font, subtle frame, and English/Japanese configuration labels.

### Environment

Minecraft **1.21.1**, **NeoForge 21.1.251+** within that Minecraft version, and FTB Quests. Built and tested with **FTB Quests 2101.1.36**, primarily in **All the Mods 10 v8.2**. Other loaders, Minecraft versions, and modpacks are not yet verified.

### Installation

Download **quest-progress-hud-0.0.9.jar** below, close the game, and put it in the instance's **mods** folder. Remove any older Quest Progress HUD jar. FTB Quests and its dependencies must already be installed. This HUD is installed on the client.

Configure through **Mods → Quest Progress HUD → Config**. Existing settings remain compatible.

### Counting and beta status

The total includes **all registered quests**; hidden, optional, repeatable, and internal quests are not excluded. Completed quests use the current synced team's completion state. The percentage is rounded to one decimal place; 0 / 0 displays 0.0%. The unchanged ATM10 v8.2 quest definitions contain 4,790 quests.

Automated build, layout, settings persistence, and sync-state tests passed. Several hours of gameplay were reported without observed issues in the primary environment. Broader modpack compatibility and multiplayer/team transitions still need in-game testing.

## 日本語

FTB Questsのチーム進捗を、件数・割合・細い緑のバーで表示する初回ベータ版です。Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.251+ 向けで、ATM10 v8.2・FTB Quests 2101.1.36を主な確認環境としています。

下のjarをダウンロードし、ゲームを終了してからインスタンスの `mods` フォルダーに入れてください。旧版のjarは外し、1つだけ入れてください。FTB Questsとその依存MODが導入済みの環境で使います。HUDはクライアントに導入します。

設定は **Mods → Quest Progress HUD → Config** から変更できます。更新は通常1秒ごとで、以前の設定は引き継げます。分母は全登録クエスト、完了数は現在同期されているチームの進捗です。数え方は変更していません。

自動テストに加え、主な確認環境で数時間の実プレイ中に問題がなかったとの報告を受けています。別パック・マルチプレイ・チーム切り替えについては、引き続き確認を進めるベータ版です。不具合はGitHub Issuesへお知らせください。
